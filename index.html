About Me

I am a Machine Learning Engineer on the Applied ML Research team at Runware, London, where I integrate, optimise, and serve image, video, audio, 3D, LLM/VLM, and multimodal models in production. My research direction is developing efficient world and multimodal models for embodied AI, with a focus on transferring spatial, temporal, and physical reasoning into compact models through knowledge distillation and efficient training and inference under strict latency, memory, and compute constraints. I am currently applying for a Master's by Research in machine learning (University of Melbourne, University of Sydney, and Monash University).

At Runware I work at the boundary between recent papers and production systems: I read new work on diffusion caching, attention kernels, context parallelism, distillation, and optimisers, reproduce it, and turn it into inference and training improvements across a platform serving 1,000+ models. Previously, I was a founding engineer of MedTalk at Faz Australia, a Computer Vision Engineer at VisionRD, and a machine learning intern at NESCOM and Bytewise.

I received my B.Sc. (Hons.) in Software Engineering from the University of Engineering & Technology (UET) Taxila, Pakistan, with a CGPA of 3.94/4.0 and the Silver Medal (second-highest aggregate in the session across all four years). Before university, I completed my HSSC (Pre-Engineering) on a full tuition-fee waiver with a stipend awarded for my Matriculation results, ranked first in Pre-Engineering in 11th grade, and was awarded a laptop under the Prime Minister's Laptop Scheme for top-performing students.
